Mt.Everest (Sagarmatha)

Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world, standing at 8,848.86 metres above sea level. It is located in the Himalayas, on the border between Nepal and China (Tibet). In Nepal, it is known as Sagarmatha.

Expore The Nepal

Nepal is a beautiful country in South Asia, famous for its majestic Himalayan mountains, rich culture, and natural beauty. It is home to Mount Everest, the highest mountain in the world. Nepal is also known for its ancient temples, peaceful monasteries, green forests, and beautiful lakes. Places like Kathmandu, Pokhara, Lumbini, and Chitwan attract visitors from around the world. Nepal is the birthplace of Lord Buddha and a country where people from different cultures and traditions live together . From the snowy peaks of the Himalayas to the green hills and plains of the Terai, Nepal offers amazing experiences for travelers. Its friendly people, delicious food, colorful festivals, and historic landmarks make it a special destination to explore.
