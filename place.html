Popular places of Nepal

Discover amazing destinations from peaceful lakes to mighty mountains.

Pokhara

Pokhara is famous for its beautiful lakes, mountain views, and peaceful surroundings.

Lumbini

Lumbini is the birthplace of Lord Buddha and a peaceful place for visitors.

Chitwan

Chitwan is known for its wildlife, green forests, and exciting jungle safaris.

Bhakatapur

Bhaktapur is a historic city filled with traditional temples, art, and culture.

Rara Lake

Rara Lake is known for its clear blue water and beautiful mountain scenery.

Ilam

Ilam is famous for its green tea gardens, rolling hills, and fresh air.
