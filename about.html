Welcome to Hidden Nepal

Nepal is a beautiful country with mountains, lakes, forests, temples, and many amazing places to explore. Every place has its own story and culture.

Hidden Nepal is a website made to introduce people to the beauty of Nepal. My goal is to help visitors discover popular destinations and learn more about the country's natural beauty and traditions.

From the peaceful lakes of Pokhara to the historic streets of Bhaktapur, there is always something new to discover in Nepal.
